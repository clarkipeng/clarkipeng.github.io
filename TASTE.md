# Taste

Read `~/.agentworld/lab/taste.md` first; this file refines it for this repo.

## Voice

- Show personality, not a list of facts.
- Keep copy enthusiastic, casual and human, with no emoji and nothing oversold ("just say I co-run ACM AI").
- No email address anywhere on the site.

## Look

- The default is the tuned minimal "K" slime look with mild colours.
- Every card keeps its own shape with its longest side at one shared cap, and the same size inside and outside the deck (it only turns upright to stack); the sticky note keeps its own small size.
- Transitions are continuous in time and space.
- On hover, the thumbnail fades into the video.
- No sound.
- Don't run the simulation on hidden pages.

## Video

- Clips follow `.context/VIDEO-SPEC.md`: 3:2 hover clips and a 16:9 long cut.
